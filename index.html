STUDENT ZONE

Your study space.
Your next step.

Simple study habits that can help students become more organised and consistent.

1

Understand before memorising

Ask yourself: “Can I explain this concept in my own words?” If not, revisit it.

2

Practise actively

Close the book and solve questions. Active recall can be more useful than simply rereading.

3

Keep a doubt list

Write down questions as they appear so you can resolve them instead of forgetting them.

4

Revise regularly

Short, repeated revision sessions can help keep important ideas fresh.
